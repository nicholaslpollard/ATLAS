# Accepted 2021–2026 signal census (development-only bridge) — 2026-09-27

The first cross-year data adapter reads **one** accepted recurrent successor development selection spanning 2021-01-01 through 2025-12-31 and projects only the original **daily LONG identity and predecision clock**, never its realized stock returns, future selector labels, or a protected window. It preserves every selected daily LONG case in a year-by-year denominator, checks signal close → following regular stock open → 09:35 structural decision, and records the frozen 28–60 day third-Friday-monthly expiry rule, the corresponding source integrity fingerprint, original opportunity ID, ticker, policy, instrument and fold.

No selected option contract, historic raw underlying price, news feature, stock/option fill or P&L is manufactured. These are later source joins. The accepted 2022 6,398 CALL histories and source casebook remain valid and reused **separately**; this six-year selection is not another 2022 acquisition or a replacement for the accepted frozen 2022 source ledger.

**2026 is explicitly visible with zero accepted DEVELOPMENT replay selections** from this source. That is deliberate: the source's 2026 segment includes a consumed protected interval and the most recent blind period. A separate independent original predecision snapshot/forward source, not an unrestricted 2026 replay, is required before options/backtests are extended there. Late-2025 signals whose next stock entry opens in 2026 also receive a precise source status and cannot silently reuse 2025 native input. Jan–September 2021 eligible stock signals stay visible even when option history is outside current rolling vendor availability.

The operator runs one local DuckDB query with four threads (configurable to 1..8), and writes a SHA-fingerprinted, immutable source census under D:-bound \`data/research/evidence\`. It emits progress from the accepted selection loader and per-year counts. It makes **zero paid provider requests**, and does not touch existing option receipts or recompute the accepted 2022 quote history. Rerun with the same accepted source reuses the identical written artifact; a source drift fails closed.

## What this unlocks

The next adapter reads the census and the existing accepted native raw stock acquisition plan to obtain exact raw 1Day entry/exit observations for the requested symbol/date pairs; it must bind native checkpoint and parquet SHAs and distinguish daily-close aggregate timing from an actual matched 16:00 bid/ask. The 2021/23/24/25 chain demand planner uses accepted stock raw entry open to select structurally, **before** opening future option EOD quote series; preexisting source receipts are checked first. Then the separately capped missing-response cache can fill only those actual needs. PIT-safe news articles attach at the original 09:35 cutoff. A later matched EOD stock/option scenario is a different clock than the original 09:35 entry. Greeks are model-derived only from observed bid/ask and validated point-in-time rate/dividend/deliverable economics.

The six-year simulation kernel and the demand cache are already on main; this step provides their missing **actual multi-year stock-signal identities** instead of relying on invented demo cases.
