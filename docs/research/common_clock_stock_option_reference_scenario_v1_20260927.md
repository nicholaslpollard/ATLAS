# Integrated common-clock stock vs option scenario V1 — 2026-09-27

This is the first **actual reusable calculation core** accepting prepared signal cases from any of **2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026**. It is not a year-specific diagnostic or data-acquisition entrypoint.

\`packages/simulation/stock_option_common_clock_v1.py\` requires original signal time, selection time before the decision, PIT news cutoff, verified stock raw EOD entry/exit marks and the *original structurally selected* option's observed entry ask and exit bid on those exact same sessions (within 30 minutes of stock marks). It refuses same-decision-day EOD entry as a 09:35 quote, expired contracts, wrong option symbol/right/expiry, unverified deliverable/multiplier, adjusted stock marks, mismatched source types, future-informed selection and out-of-order times. When the option source is absent it keeps the signal and reports explicit no-data rather than dropping the case or selecting a future-liquid alternate.

For illustration it applies one identical fixed $10,000 budget to the stock and the long option alternative. Stock entry/exit apply explicit basis-point adverse slippage. Option uses observed ask-at-entry, bid-at-exit plus explicit fees and requires a verified standard 100-share deliverable. The results are **side-price EOD reference scenario values**; those snapshots are not certified executable historical fills. Individual per-case calculations are not an overlapping-position portfolio account, do not model a discretionary stop/target intraday path, and do not promote any strategy. The outputs have no paper, live, or protected-holdout-as-fresh authority.

\`compare_cases\` gives a six-year census including per-year zero rows, all original cases and missing-source status; never substitutes an available-pair median as a full-cohort return. \`scripts/run_stock_option_common_clock_v1.py --cases <prepared.json>\` runs purely locally when the separate source adapter has supplied the audited prepared case schema. Source strings and SHA hints provided by an arbitrary caller are not by themselves proof of source authenticity: the adapter must check original local files/receipts before constructing \`SameClockCase\` records.

This next step **is** a common replay kernel, but producing the actual historical per-year results requires the adapter to join the accepted D:-bound options and news to the C:-bound historical native raw daily stock marks at both later entry/exit dates. The original 2022 casebook intentionally has raw 09:30 open only and therefore cannot supply those same-clock later stock marks yet; do not use its original open as an option EOD entry. The 2021 early interval remains outside the rolling five-year Starter window without a different licensed source. Actual 09:35 trading requires separate timestamped option quote/size evidence. The proper integration priority is the **multi-year source adapter and demand-driven, credit-capped missing-series cache**, not another isolated one-year study.

**No paid MarketData requests are performed in this PR.** Local replay/tuning must remain fully offline; missing-source preparation is separately consented with durable receipts and bounded credit/spend. The day's <2,000 remaining allowance must not be presumed current by the execution worker.
