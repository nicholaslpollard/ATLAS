## New script and exact scope

\`scripts/run_marketdata_2022_full_bulk_v1.py\` is **one operator-run program** that finishes the rest of the original frozen additive 2022 monthly-CALL source research cohort, shards 26–70 (1,772 physical query keys). Existing 0–25, original separate 36-query 2022 three/month pilot and original 2025 eleven-contract quote pilot remain byte-for-byte unchanged. It is not a fetch-the-whole-exchange plan.

1. **Before any new provider read:** verify the original 36-query accepted source once and validate original frozen 71-shard/2,812-key census, physical key uniqueness, PIT source SHA and per-shard bindings. Freeze any missing shard 26–70 source using the accepted native LONG daily replay loaded only once (DuckDB threads 4); native raw OPEN remains the stock price basis. No outcomes, future option EOD volume or protected holdout reads can enter historical contract selection.
2. **Parallel historical chains:** inspect original receipts and exact 404 proofs for each remaining shard. Only pending, original distinct shard plans may run paid acquisition. Process 4 shards concurrently initially, increasing by two up to eight concurrent independent shard jobs after fully completed waves. Each original shard uses the proven per-request exclusive durable attempt-before-GET, receipt+raw-body SHA, 8 MiB response bound, original stock source binding, bounded 40 exact calls and no blind retry. A worker failure joins the other already in-flight wave jobs, preserves all original evidence, and stops before any new wave or quote plan. Last reported provider remaining and original observed charges govern next dispatch. Do not launch another authenticated MarketData campaign concurrently.
3. **Strict source barrier:** after every original shard has an intact successful chain or a strictly proved exact-request 404/no_data gap and zero pending, freeze the same existing V2 PIT CALL envelope through shard 70: all actual prior-session CALL strikes within each opportunity's original ±8% raw OPEN window at its originally captured 28–60-DTE monthly expiry. Distinct exact OCC symbol \`from/to\` histories are deduplicated across all opportunities and all years of that specific contract's required range. This is not all expirations or all intraday contracts.
4. **Parallel historical quote series:** use the existing physical 2022 full-year-to-expiry exact quote cache, so all 2,725 earlier series are reused and only missing exact histories are downloaded. Run 1,024-GET bounded chunks, initially 16 network workers and adapt 16→20→24 on measured throughput; reduce worker count if measured full-chunk rate degrades materially. The provider's **50 account-wide** simultaneous GET limit remains a ceiling, not an instruction to saturate it. Each response writes original intent/body/receipt and checks correct identity, bounds, SHA and provider headers immediately. No reissued request after timeout, malformed raw payload or quarantine. The quote stage never overlaps paid chain workers.
5. **One authorized cost envelope:** default at most 12,000 *new* original GETs and 8,000 *observed* credits across at most two separate daily credit windows. Monitor minimum-free D: and 120-GiB candidate-cache quota before each bounded set. Provider-reported charges override request-count estimates. If provider headroom approaches the 200-credit reserve, optionally wait until the next official daily reset (09:30 America/New_York; +90 seconds slack), without making periodic API probes or reusing old debit balances as new. Never wait simply to exceed the operator's aggregate 8,000-credit authorization. In-flight concurrency can cause small post-response overshoot; this is an observed-credit stopping target, not a guaranteed invoice maximum.
6. **Resume and visibility:** exclusive D: \`acquisition.lock\` prevents a second full-bulk run; run and latest progress checkpoints are stored under \`data/options/manifests/marketdata_2022_full_bulk_v1/\`. A clean stop releases its lock and exact physical receipts make a subsequent identical command skip paid completions. A killed process intentionally leaves its lock; verify the process and original uncertain attempt before manually clearing it. Console reports source waves, worker counts/GETs per second, quote chunks, exact pending and complete series, provider charges/remaining and actual D: before/after. Do not confuse a complete staged-source closeout with strategy/PAPER/LIVE approval.

**Performance baseline:** previous 16-worker quote stage downloaded 1,016 new exact series in 358.328 seconds, about 2.84 GET/s after the census and other overhead. Historical chain source stage was previously sequential, often about two seconds per GET. The new source shard concurrency is the material change; a high CPU or SSD utilization number is not the performance goal. Measure source keys and usable quote observations per minute, cost, and whether duplicate GETs remain zero. No evidence yet that 24 workers beats 16 on the user's network; the adaptive policy measures rather than assumes.

**Remaining research boundaries:** the separate original three/month 2022 source requires its own opportunity-to-quote integration; 2023–2025 accepted source expansion requires new frozen eligible cohorts; missing expirations/adjusted deliverables and 09:35 historical intraday quote data are separate sources.
