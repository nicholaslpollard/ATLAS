### Greeks are a design requirement, not an optional future flourish

The existing `packages/simulation/historical_option_greeks_v1.py` solves observed option MID implied volatility and derives delta, gamma, daily theta, vega, rho, but it **requires** an observed, valid historical two-sided bid/ask; a timestamp-matched raw-as-traded stock price; exact OCC strike/right/expiry; independently verified 100-share deliverable; and dated risk-free/dividend inputs with identifiers. If any are absent, record missing IV/Greeks with a machine-readable reason. The current model is a European-equivalent BSM research sensitivity, not full American early exercise, not historical exchange implied Greeks, and not an observed executable bid/ask. Long/short positions also require actual original spread, liquidity, commissions, slippage, assignments and corporate-action-adjusted deliverables to simulate defensible cash outcomes. **Never backdate EOD option quote observations to 09:35 ET.** Retimed later-EOD stock and option scenarios can be researched separately; a faithful original 09:35 options entry requires independently qualified timestamped historical intraday option bid/ask.
