Official ThetaData documentation was reviewed directly against ATLAS's pinned Python-library transport, independent of live entitlement testing. [The historical EOD endpoint](https://thetadata.net/docs/operations_python/option_history_eod.html) is a **17:15 ET generated report**, not a historical 09:35 fill or guaranteed end-of-session tradable quote, with distinct `created` and `last_trade` timestamps. The frozen worker currently permits ONLY the three 09:35 quote/OI/binomial-Greeks read methods, so the Free EOD method is not yet integrated or authorized for provider calls.

The [at-time quote](https://thetadata.net/docs/operations_python/option_at_time_quote.html) returns the last OPRA NBBO at an ET decision timestamp. ThetaData warns that sub-minute timestamps combined with all expirations can become very slow; the exact 09:35 and 10:00 minute-boundary predicates are appropriate for candidate retrieval, subject to quote staleness, historical listing/adjustment, and executable-size validation.
